Most email analytics focus on vanity metrics. PostMTA tracks what actually matters for deliverability.

**Delivery Metrics**
Overall delivery rate (target: 98%+), ISP-specific delivery rates, bounce breakdown by type, complaint rate tracking.

**Engagement Metrics**
Open rate (with privacy considerations), click rate by campaign, unsubscribe rate, list decay rate.

**Reputation Metrics**
IP reputation score (0-100), domain reputation by ISP, authentication pass rates, spam verdict rates.

**Trend Analysis**
See metrics over time. Identify problems before they become crises.

**Custom Reports**
Build custom reports for different stakeholders. Export to CSV or connect via API.

**Alerting**
Threshold-based alerts notify you of problems immediately.
