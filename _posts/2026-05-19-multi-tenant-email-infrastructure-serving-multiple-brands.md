PostMTA supports multi-tenant architecture for agencies and companies managing email for multiple brands.

## Features

Sub-accounts: Separate credentials and IPs per client
Whitelabel: Custom tracking domains per brand
Unified Analytics: Aggregate or per-client reporting
Role-Based Access: Client cannot see other clients data

## Use Cases

Email agencies: Manage 50+ clients from one dashboard
Multi-brand companies: Separate reputations per product line
