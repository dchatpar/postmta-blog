Mailgun and PostMTA are both email infrastructure solutions with different approaches.

## Mailgun

SaaS API-first, per-email pricing ($0.00080/email), shared IP pool, 24-hour analytics delay.

## PostMTA

SaaS or self-hosted, flat pricing unlimited sends, dedicated IPs with warmup, real-time analytics, enterprise features.

## When to Choose Mailgun

Low volume (under 10K/mo), simple transactional needs, prefer pure SaaS.

## When to Choose PostMTA

High volume (100K+/mo), need dedicated IPs, want real-time analytics, multi-tenant support.
