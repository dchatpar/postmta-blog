Cold email can be effective when done right. PostMTA provides the infrastructure to send cold email at scale.

## Best Practices

1. Warm up first - Start with 50 emails/day
2. Authentic content - Personalize, avoid spam words
3. Valid list - Verify emails before sending
4. Clear unsubscribe - CAN-SPAM required
5. Separate IPs - Dedicated for cold campaigns
