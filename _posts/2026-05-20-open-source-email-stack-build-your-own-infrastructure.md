Own your email infrastructure with an open source stack built around PostMTA.

## The Stack

MTA: PostMTA (SMTP relay, queue management)
Webmail: Roundcube or Roundup
Groupware: SOGo (calendar, contacts, mail)

## Why Self-Host?

- No per-email pricing
- Full data ownership
- Custom branding
- Compliance (GDPR, HIPAA)
- No vendor lock-in
