PostMTA webhooks notify your application when emails are delivered, bounced, clicked, or cause complaints.

## Events

message.delivered, message.bounced, message.complained, message.clicked, message.opened

## Use Cases

Trigger follow-up emails after delivery. Update CRM when recipient engages. Pause campaigns if complaints spike. Remove unsubscribes in real-time.
